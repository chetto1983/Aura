<objective>
Prove the rerank win and stop it from ever hurting: an eval harness (nDCG@10/Recall@5/MRR,
vector vs vector+rerank), a non-monotonic guard, a CI-green live tier, and the >=85% coverage gate.
Self-learning stays explicitly OUT.

Purpose: Spike 070 showed rerank is non-monotonic (the "back-to-box" case demoted the right answer).
This plan is the regression gate: a labeled judgment set quantifies the lift and the guard ensures
rerank only applies when it improves. It also closes the phase's quality bars (coverage, CI-green
live tiers) and records that self-learning is deferred.

Output: internal/eval retrieval harness + pure metrics + judgments, the rerank_guard wired into
Retrieve/GraphRAG, a CI job that runs the live tiers with exported env, the eval doc, and a passing
make coverage.
</objective>
