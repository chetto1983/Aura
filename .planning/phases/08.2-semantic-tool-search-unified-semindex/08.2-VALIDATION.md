## CI vs Live-only

- **CI (default + race + integration tags), no sidecar — free & deterministic:** Req-1, Req-2 (deterministic corpus gate w/ precomputed fixtures), Req-3, Req-4 (unit + equivalence), Req-5a/b/c, Req-6 (unit error-surface), Req-7, Req-8 (no-ANN assertion + benchmark).
- **Live-only (operator-authorized, NOT default CI):** `TestReasoningClassifierLive` (tag `reasoning_live`, granite :8081); live confirm of the Req-2 corpus + the Req-8 ≤ 20ms latency at real corpus size; the `reasoningstore`/`toolselectstore` Neo4j round-trip (tag `reasoning_live` per shipped convention). The paid `live_e2e` (OpenRouter) tier is **not** required for this phase's gates.
