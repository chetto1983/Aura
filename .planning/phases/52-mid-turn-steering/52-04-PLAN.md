**The tracer.** A steer typed into a live cockpit run reaches the model at the next round boundary, is
echoed on the wire, and is persisted where it landed — end to end, production quality, in one commit.

inbox the same object. STEER-01 and STEER-03 are only meaningful together — a route with no
persistence loses the steer on reload; persistence with no route is unreachable.
