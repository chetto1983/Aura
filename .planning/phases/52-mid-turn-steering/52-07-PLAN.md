typing and sending redirects the turn instead of doing nothing.

"the UI echoes that the turn was redirected" had no owner either.

unreachable, and 52-08's human-check questions — *"Does the composer accept the message (no 409, no
dead input)?"*, *"Does the steer appear in the thread… after a page reload?"* — cannot be answered
yes by any code in the tree.
