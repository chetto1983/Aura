<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** lexical hit on the word *custom* in `45.1-CONTEXT.md` D-101 — *"`egress_policy.go` (re-attached as a custom `http.RoundTripper`)"*.

**Noun now primary:** the MCP client session. Before this phase, "an MCP connection" was two
unrelated concrete types (`*mcp.Client` for stdio, `*mcp.HTTPClient` for streamable HTTP) joined
by a hand-written `Transport` interface. After it, both transports converge on one
`*sdkmcp.ClientSession`.

**Decision: `no-change`.**

The `http.RoundTripper` half of the signal is not a derived→chosen transition at all —
`newHardenedHTTPClient` was already a `*http.Client` before this phase and is used verbatim; the
word "custom" describes what it always was. The *real* generalization the signal gestures at is
the transport convergence, and it does not need a promote either: `OpenSDKSession` already takes
the general shape (a `ManagedServer` plus an `EgressPolicy`) and branches internally on
`Classify`, so there is no singular assumption left embedded in a caller. The 13 `cmd/aura` files
and the agent bridge all call the same general function.

**What would force a promote later:** a third transport class arriving (a remote third-party
server needing `OAuthHandler`, currently OPT-OUT in COVERAGE.md). At that point `Classify`'s
two-way branch becomes a three-way one and `SessionOptions` grows an auth field — promote
`OpenSDKSession`'s branch into a transport-strategy lookup then, not now.
</assumption_delta_decision>
