<objective>
Re-point every remaining consumer of the bespoke MCP client at the SDK session, delete the
bespoke client, and install a guard that makes "deleted, not left dormant" checkable rather
than aspirational.

Purpose: this is the plan that restores D-111's invariant — **no commit after this one leaves two
MCP clients in the tree** — and it is the plan MCPC-03 is measured against.

D-101: this plan completes the deletion half of D-101's scope (client + transport + reconnect +
keepalive, deleted rather than left dormant), while its preservation half — `ssrf.go`,
`transport_ssrf.go`, `egress_policy.go`, `managed_config*.go`, `classify.go`, `tool_error.go`,
`domain_outcome.go`, `observability.go`, `redact.go` — is re-attached on the SDK in plans 45.1-01
and 45.1-02 and must still be intact when this plan's deletions land.
Output: 1,291 LOC of non-test transport code deleted, ~2,500 LOC of its tests deleted, 16
`cmd/aura` files and `internal/config` re-pointed, and `TestNoHandRolledJSONRPCFraming`.

**Measured correction the executor must carry forward:** ROADMAP and CONTEXT both say
"≈900 LOC of tests" are deleted. Counted directly at HEAD, the real figure is **≈3,750**
(`internal/mcp`: 2,503 across twelve files; `internal/agent/mcptools`: 1,244 across four files,
already removed in plan 45.1-02). The 85% owned-surface coverage floor is therefore under four
times the pressure the roadmap assumed. Task 3 exists because of that number.
</objective>
