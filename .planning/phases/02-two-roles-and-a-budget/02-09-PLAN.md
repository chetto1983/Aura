<objective>
Build the account-wide reconciliation surface the approved UI-SPEC specifies: five KPI tiles with
sparklines and prior-period deltas, a Top-Identities-by-spend ranked list, and the over-allocation
advisory — all fetched server-side with the management credential and rendered with no charting
library.

Purpose: the human asked for this shape explicitly, with a screenshot of OpenRouter's own activity
dashboard, and the UI-SPEC was revised post-approval to specify it panel by panel against the
measured API rather than against the screenshot's literal content. It is additive: nothing here
edits a cap, and the binding CRED-03/CRED-06 controls stay in the Credit panel plan `02-08` built.

**This plan integrates three OpenRouter capabilities that `COVERAGE.md` originally opted out of.**
That file has been amended with the reason, the date and a reclassification log — `GET /keys` for
the ranked list's `external_user`/`label`/`usage`, `GET /credits` for the over-allocation trigger,
and `POST /analytics/query` for the five tiles. The in-band-ledger rule that motivated two of the
three original opt-outs is unchanged and still enforced: this surface is reconciliation only.

Output: three reconciliation calls in `internal/openrouterprovision`, one server-side aggregating
endpoint, and a zero-dependency dashboard.
</objective>
