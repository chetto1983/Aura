<objective>
Give the provisioning saga a credential leg and the reverse saga a revocation leg: every new
identity is minted its own OpenRouter key at a zero cap and stored encrypted, and every removed
identity's key is revoked and the revocation verified.

Purpose: plan `02-01` proved a stored key reaches a turn, and plan `02-03` built the four verbs.
Neither put a key in the store on the documented path. Until this plan lands, every identity is
provisioned without a credential and is refused every turn — which is correct behaviour and
useless product. This is the leg that makes the phase's own closing run possible.

Output: `sagaStepOpenRouterKey` on both sagas, with compensation on the forward side and verified
404 on the reverse, the management credential allowlisted in `aura.settings`, and the composition
root wired so neither port is nil in production.
</objective>
