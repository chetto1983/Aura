<objective>
Make every capability denial answerable after the fact: who was refused, which capability, at
which route, when — recorded at the one gate all of them pass through, and readable from the
admin surface that already exists.

Purpose: RBAC-10 is the requirement the closing live run reads back ("B attempts to create an
identity and to remove one, and both are refused and readable afterwards out of the audit trail
by their own query"). Today `RequireCapability` writes an `http.Error(w, "forbidden", 403)` and
nothing else — a grep for a denial ledger across `internal/identity` and `internal/agui` finds
`password_reset.go`'s recovery events and nothing for capabilities. This is a real gap, measured
rather than assumed.

Output: `aura.capability_denials`, a recorder wired into `RequireCapability` behind a
consumer-side port, and a fifth leg on the audit feed's existing UNION so the cockpit reads
denials with no new endpoint.
</objective>
