<objective>
Give the admin the two controls the phase promises: remove an identity, and set what an identity
may spend — both through HTTP, both idempotent, both gated on the right capability. And fix the
ledger those controls read from, which cannot currently hold the numbers it is asked to hold.

Purpose: D-08 splits the job three ways — OpenRouter enforces, Aura's in-band ledger displays and
refuses, and the provider reconciles. This plan builds the display-and-refuse half. In building
it, the plan collides with a measured defect: `cost_usd` is `numeric(10, 4)` and the per-call cost
measured on 2026-09-08 is `0.000004158`. Every cheap call currently stores as `0.0000`. CLAUDE.md's
NOT MY WORK rule makes fixing that part of this plan rather than a note for later.

Output: a widened money column with a migration test, a per-identity period-spend query over the
in-band ledger, `GET`/`POST /api/admin/identities/{id}/credit`, `DELETE /api/admin/identities/{id}`
over the existing reverse saga, both registered for idempotency and mounted on the right gate.
</objective>

  <context>
    A schema change to two money columns on tables shipped features already read and write, whose
    down direction loses data. `gate="blocking-human"` rather than `blocking`: auto-mode would
    auto-select the first option, and this is a defect found at planning time that CONTEXT.md and

  </context>
  <decision>
    A defect found at planning time, not anticipated by CONTEXT.md or RESEARCH.md, and it changes
    this plan's shape.

    **Measured:** `aura.cache_metrics.cost_usd` is `numeric(10, 4)`
    (`internal/db/migrations/0007_cache_metrics.up.sql`) and `aura.conversations.total_cost_usd`
    is `numeric(10, 4)` (`internal/db/migrations/0005_conversations.up.sql:19`). No later
    migration alters either — grep over the whole migrations directory returns only those two
    files plus `0047`, which references the column in a trigger without changing its type.

    **The number they must hold:** `02-CONTEXT.md` M-09 records four calls at `0.000004158` each,
    summing to a provider-reported `0.000016632` — "Aura's own ledger and the provider agree
    exactly". At scale 4, `0.000004158` stores as `0.0000`. The agreement M-09 measured is an
    agreement between two numbers computed in memory; the moment either is persisted through this
    column it becomes zero.

    **Why it lands here:** D-08 makes this ledger the source of the displayed balance and of
    CRED-05's pre-flight refusal. A ledger that reads zero for every cheap call reports every
    identity as having spent nothing, which makes the cockpit's "spend" figure wrong in the
    direction that looks fine.

    **The change:** `ALTER COLUMN cost_usd TYPE numeric(20, 10)` on both columns. Widening is
    lossless and needs no data rewrite. The `.down.sql` narrows back to `numeric(10, 4)`, and
    **that direction loses data** — every sub-`0.0001` value written while the wider type was in
    force rounds to zero on the way back. That is what makes this `one-way` and why it is a
    checkpoint rather than a task.

    **Blast radius:** `aura cache-stats` reads `AggregateCacheMetricsSince`;
    `internal/agent/turn_usage.go` writes it; `internal/runner/runner_persist.go` calls the write.
    None of them needs a code change for a wider column — but each should be re-run, and the
    `db_integration` suites for `internal/db` and `internal/agent` are the check.
  </decision>
  <options>
    - **Approve `numeric(20, 10)`.** Ten decimal places covers the measured `0.000004158` with
      three digits of headroom and ten integer digits for the account-scale totals.
    - **Approve a different scale.** Name it. The constraint is that it must hold the smallest
      per-call cost this deployment actually sees, and 4 decimals demonstrably does not.
    - **Redirect: do not widen; store cost in micro-dollars as an integer instead.** Exact by
      construction and immune to scale questions, but it changes the column's type rather than
      its precision, touches every reader and writer, and is a larger change than this plan.
    - **Redirect: accept the rounding and display a different number.** Requires naming what that
      number is; it cannot be the provider's, because M-07's 30-40s lag is why D-08 chose the
      in-band ledger in the first place.
  </options>
  <recommendation>
    Approve `numeric(20, 10)`. It is the smallest change that makes the measured number
    representable, and M-09's exact ledger/provider agreement — the phase's best evidence that
    the in-band approach works — is only true if the column can hold it.
  </recommendation>
  <resume-signal>Reply "approve 20,10", name a different scale, or reply "micro-dollars" / "accept rounding" to take one of the two redirects</resume-signal>
</task>
