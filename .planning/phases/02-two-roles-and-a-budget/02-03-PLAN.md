<objective>
Build the OpenRouter Provisioning-API client: mint a key at a zero cap, read one, change its cap
and reset interval, and revoke it verifiably. Four verbs, one new package, tested entirely
against `httptest` — no daemon, no network, no real credential.

Purpose: the cap is the phase's entire enforcement mechanism (D-07), and it lives on the
provider's key rather than in a table of ours. That makes this package the boundary where every
measured provider behaviour either gets encoded or gets rediscovered in production. Two of those
behaviours are counter-intuitive and are the reason this is a plan rather than a task: a key at
`limit: 0` is refused with HTTP **403**, not the 402 one would guess; and a `limit` field that
marshals away when it is zero mints an **uncapped** key, which is the precise opposite of what
CRED-02 asks for.

Output: `internal/openrouterprovision` with `MintKey`, `GetKey`, `PatchKey`, `DeleteKey`, a
sentinel set that distinguishes exhausted from revoked from missing, and an httptest fixture per
verb built from the response shapes `02-OPENROUTER-API.md` transcribed from the live account.
</objective>
