<objective>
Introduce ONE canonical transport+trust classifier — `internal/mcp/classify.go` exporting
`Classify(ManagedServer) (serverType, trust string, err error)` — and collapse the scattered
per-site checks in the `internal/mcp` core package (`managed_config.go`, `transport.go`) onto it.
This closes F-027 (mixed `url`+`command` silently resolved to stdio) and F-013 (a bare-URL remote
auto-promoted to a runnable `TrustRemoteHTTP` with zero operator approval) at their two highest-priority
call sites, and establishes the classifier that Plans 38-04/38-05/38-06 migrate the remaining call
sites onto.

Purpose: MCPH-01 requires a single classifier governing validation/trust-norm/eligibility/mounting/opening
so the same shape is never re-decided (differently) in nine places. MCPH-02 requires an untrusted remote
to be BLOCKED, not runnable. Both are security-gating input-validation boundaries (ASVS V5).
Output: `classify.go` + `classify_test.go` (new), with `managed_config.go` and `transport.go` migrated.

<assumption_delta_decision>
Noun promoted to primary: the transport/trust classifier. Decision: **promote** — `Classify` becomes the
single source of truth; the pre-existing scattered decision functions (`normalizedServerType`,
`NormalizedTrust`, and the independent copies in `manager/runtime.go`, `mount.go`, `mcp_status.go`) are
demoted to thin callers of `Classify` (this plan does the two core-package sites; 38-04/05/06 do the rest).
Rationale: D-01's stated intent — a true single source of truth — is exactly the "many scattered classifiers
→ one" consolidation the assumption-delta detector flags; promote is correct, add-alongside would re-create
the divergent-copies bug this phase closes.
</assumption_delta_decision>
</objective>
