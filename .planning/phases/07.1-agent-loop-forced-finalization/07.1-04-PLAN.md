<objective>
Complete the loop-hardening: (Req#3) a counter-gated one-shot recovery turn — the FIRST trip (dedup OR budget) injects a `user`-role nudge and runs one more turn before finalizing; a second trip of any kind goes straight to `finalize()`. (Req#4) recovery + finalize run OUTSIDE the step budget (never `ConsumeStep`; ceiling `max_steps+2`). (Req#5) inside `finalize()`, retry once on empty/error then emit a deterministic Italian stub (D-09) digesting the gathered tool results — no path emits empty prose. Also wire the real `branchConsumed`/`Remaining()` counts into the `Build` call (replacing the plan-02 placeholder) so the `<budget>` block is live.

Purpose: Plan 03 routes trips straight to a single finalize. This plan inserts the recover-not-abort turn (CrewAI #1656 counter, no latch), guarantees finalize never costs a budget step, and guarantees a non-empty answer even when finalize itself fails. Implements SPEC Req#3/#4/#5 + ACs 5/6/7, INFRA-03.
Output: The recovery counter + budget-bypass re-entry, the retry/stub fallback, the live `<budget>` counts, and the recovery/ceiling/fallback tests.
</objective>
