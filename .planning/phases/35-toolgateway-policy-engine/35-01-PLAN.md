<objective>
Deliver D-02: the trustworthy `Mutating` classification for the three action-multiplexed tools (`skill`, `task`, `swarm_spawn`) plus the in-process `classify(spec, rawArgs) → scoring.RiskTier` monotone de-escalator that the Gateway's `Decide` will consume. This is the foundational, DB-free floor of the phase — the fail-closed default is always mutating; the classifier only *lowers* explicitly-enumerated read actions to `Safe`; everything unknown/unparseable saturates upward to `Risky`.

Purpose: A trustworthy `Mutating` flag is a HARD PREREQUISITE of the reservation (D-01a) and the crash-orphan reconciler (D-01d) — you cannot safely reserve or reconcile a mutating orphan without it. This plan establishes GATE-01's classification substrate and GATE-03's fail-closed floor.

Output: `internal/gateway/classify.go` (+ fuzz/table tests), the optional boot-guard, and the 3 `Mutating:true` Spec edits. `internal/scoring` stays untouched.
</objective>
