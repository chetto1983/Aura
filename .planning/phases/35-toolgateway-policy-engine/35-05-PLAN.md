<objective>
Deliver D-01d (GATE-03 durability + GATE-04 recovery): the crash-orphan reconciler that closes a `start`-without-`end` reservation older than a grace window by APPENDING an `end{status='error', meta.indeterminate}` fact — never re-invoking a mutating orphan (its side effect may already have fired). Mirror `conversations.Sweeper`'s leak-clean Start/Stop lifecycle and the `orphan_scan.go` age-grace idiom. This makes GATE-04's durable state machine recovery-complete: an in-flight reservation left by a crash between reserve and Execute is resolved conservatively, and a trustworthy `Mutating` flag (D-02, plan 35-01) is the hard prerequisite that lets the reconciler distinguish "safe to re-invoke" from "mark indeterminate." Because 35-04's unified reserve makes an operator-approved-and-resumed mutating call produce the SAME single start∧¬end shape as an auto-allowed call, the reconciler handles both uniformly — no approve-path special-casing.

**Data-integrity guard (checker WARNING 4):** the reconciler and the tool's own real completion insert to the SAME unique key (conversation_id, request_id, tool_call_id, event_kind='end') with `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING` (first-writer-wins, 35-04 contract). To prevent the reconciler from racing a legitimately slow (non-crashed) mutating tool and permanently misrecording its success as an indeterminate error, the reconciler's EFFECTIVE grace is computed to exceed the maximum possible single-run tool-execution lifetime (`effectiveGrace = max(reservationOrphanGrace, maxToolExecWindow + reservationOrphanMargin)`), so a start∧¬end older than the effective grace is a PROVABLE crash-orphan. A pre-append `GetEnd` re-check closes the residual list→append window, and the honest residual (Aura has no hard per-tool ctx deadline by default) is handled benignly — see the truths.

Purpose: GATE-03/04 require the durable reservation state machine to support recovery. Append-only means you cannot UPDATE the orphan to "aborted"; you append an `end` fact. The reconciler is conservative: mutating orphans are marked indeterminate for the model/operator, never blindly re-run, and it never overwrites a still-running-or-slow tool's real outcome.

Output: `internal/gateway/reconcile.go` (Reconciler + reconcileOrphans + `reservationOrphanGrace`/`reservationOrphanMargin` consts + `effectiveGrace`), the serve-root Start/Stop wiring with the resolved `maxToolExecWindow`, and the `db_integration` + goleak proof tier plus the unit invariant proof.
</objective>

| reconciler → append-only ledger | The reconciler can only append; it must not mutate or hide the orphan. |
