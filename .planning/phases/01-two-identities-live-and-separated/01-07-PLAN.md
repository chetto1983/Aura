<objective>
Measure the one thing this phase assumes and has never checked, before anything is built on it.

`EnforceFirstLogin` (`internal/agui/onboarding_provision.go:204-210`) marks every
saga-provisioned identity as owing a forced password change AND a mandatory TOTP *enrollment*.
`scripts/agui_smoke.sh:267-304` handles TOTP *verify* against an identity that is already
enrolled. Nothing in this repository drives a freshly provisioned identity through first login,
so the wire contract of the enrollment half is unmeasured — RESEARCH.md records it as
Assumption A2 and as Open Question 2, and it recommends reading the plugin's exported API
before any harness design commits to it.

Purpose: the answer decides what E2E-02's "reaches a useful conversation from zero, with no
manual step outside the documented path" is allowed to claim. If enrollment can only be
completed by a human scanning a QR code, then a headless closing harness cannot drive it and
the requirement's central claim has to be restated — a conclusion that is cheap now and
expensive after five plans have shipped. The read needs no stack, no Docker and no prior plan;
it is minutes of work and it has no business sitting at wave 4.

Output: a recorded contract with the symbol or endpoint that establishes it, and the last
undispositioned question in RESEARCH.md closed with that contract's verdict. Q1 and Q3 needed no
measurement — they were editorial, and their dispositions were appended to RESEARCH.md at plan
time rather than scheduled here, so the artifact is already honest about its own state when this
plan is read and Task 2 has exactly one question left to close.
</objective>
