<objective>
Pay the Slice-1 reasoning debt (amendment #57): land the additive reasoning data-plane so the full CoT streams live, masking the longest part of a DeepSeek-V4 turn. Today reasoning deltas are DROPPED at the wire (`wireChunk.Delta` decodes only content+tool_calls) — the operator sees NOTHING until the first content token. This plan threads reasoning from the SSE wire through `llm.Chunk` and `agent.LLMResponse` to a reasoning chunk Event, all additive, zero refactor, zero reverse imports.

Purpose: amendment #57 + the Slice-8 acceptance at prd.md ~2753 (REASONING_* dual-field, #33) require reasoning to reach the AG-UI translator (Plan 12-06) and the CLI renderer. Without this data-plane the DGX/`local-only` deployment never emits reasoning and the latency-masking surface stays empty. Reasoning is STREAM-ONLY: never persisted to conversation_turns, no semantic parsing, byte-per-byte passthrough (#33 unchanged).

Output: `wireChunk.Delta` accept-both + immediate emission, `llm.Chunk.Reasoning`, `agent.LLMResponse.Reasoning`, the `reasoningChunkEvent` mirror + consume-loop case, two golden SSE dual-field fixtures, and round-trip + immediacy + no-leak tests.
</objective>
