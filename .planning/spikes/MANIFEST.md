**Session-10 requirements (emerged from spikes 040-042, binding for any adaptive-reasoning/LLM-routing planning):**

- Do not conflate AdaptThink with AutoThink. AdaptThink is training/RL prior art and released model-family signal; it is not an Aura runtime policy dependency.
