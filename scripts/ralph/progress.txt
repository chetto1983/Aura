* Swarm uses PER-ROLE MaxToolCalls (1-5 range, see internal/swarm/plan.go:245
  roleMaxToolCalls and internal/swarmtools/tools.go:427 roleMaxToolCalls).

  {"reply": str, "elapsed_ms": int, "llm_calls": int, "tool_calls": int, "tokens": int}
  cmd/probe_chat + cmd/chat depend on it. Diff against current shape before
  committing.

  (today via agent.Task.CompleteOnDeadline). Find the analog in agentruntime
  or add it.

* No new dependencies. No protocol changes. agentruntime.Run today emits
  streaming events but accepts a non-streaming ChatClient; the
  no_stream_client.go wrapper (~30 LOC) lets non-streaming callers reuse it
  without forcing streaming where it wasn't before.
